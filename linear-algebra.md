
## What is a scalar?

A scalar is a real number.

$$
x \in \mathbb{R}
$$

---

## What is a vector?

A vector is an ordered tuple of real numbers.

$$
\mathbf{v} = (v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n)
$$

where

$$
v_i \in \mathbb{R}
$$
